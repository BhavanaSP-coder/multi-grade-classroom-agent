Subject: Social Studies
Grade: 5
Unit: Our Country India and Local Government

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Names the main physical features of India: mountains, plains, plateaus, coasts, rivers.
- Names the national symbols: flag, anthem, emblem, animal, bird.
- Explains the work of the gram panchayat or local body in simple terms.
- Shows respect for diversity in languages, food and dress across India.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Physical map tour: trace the Himalayas, the Ganga plains and the Deccan plateau on a map.
2. Symbol cards: match national symbols with their names.
3. Mock gram sabha: children discuss and vote on a village problem.
4. Diversity chart: list languages and foods from different states.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Name two rivers of India and one mountain range.
- What is the work of the gram panchayat?
- Draw the national flag and colour it correctly.

Teacher notes (demo)
Connect the gram panchayat lesson to the village; invite a panchayat member if possible.
