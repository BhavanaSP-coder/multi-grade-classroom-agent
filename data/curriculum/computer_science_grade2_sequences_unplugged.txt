Subject: Computer Science
Grade: 2
Unit: Following Instructions in Order (Sequences)

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Follows step-by-step instructions in the correct order.
- Puts the steps of a daily task in order (brushing teeth, making tea).
- Understands that computers also follow instructions in order.
- Uses a mouse and keyboard if a device is available.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Robot teacher: children give the teacher step-by-step instructions to walk to the door.
2. Order the steps: picture cards of planting a seed arranged in order.
3. Sequence dance: follow a sequence of moves (clap, jump, turn).
4. Mouse practice on a device if available.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Put the steps of brushing teeth in order.
- Oral: What happens if steps are in the wrong order?
- Give three instructions to move a friend to the board.

Teacher notes (demo)
Unplugged activities teach computational thinking even without computers.
