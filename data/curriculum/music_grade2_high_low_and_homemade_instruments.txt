Subject: Music
Grade: 2
Unit: High and Low Sounds and Homemade Instruments

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Tells high sounds from low sounds.
- Makes a simple shaker or drum from waste materials.
- Plays a steady beat on a homemade instrument.
- Sings in a group, starting and stopping together.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Bottle music: fill bottles with different amounts of water and tap them to hear high and low notes.
2. Shaker making: seeds inside a closed bottle or tin.
3. Band time: groups keep the beat while the class sings.
4. Stop and start: the class follows the teacher's hand signals.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Which bottle made the highest sound?
- Play the beat of a song on your shaker.
- Observation: does the group start and stop together?

Teacher notes (demo)
Link high and low sounds with Science lessons on sound.
