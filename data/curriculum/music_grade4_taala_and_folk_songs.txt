Subject: Music
Grade: 4
Unit: Taala (Rhythm Cycles) and Folk Songs

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Counts beats in a rhythm cycle such as an 8-beat cycle.
- Keeps taala with claps and waves while singing.
- Sings local folk songs and explains what they are about.
- Performs in a small group.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. 8-beat count: clap and wave to count an 8-beat cycle together.
2. Folk song collection: children learn a folk song from home and teach it to the class.
3. Rhythm circle: each child adds a beat pattern in turn.
4. Small group performance at the assembly.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Clap an 8-beat cycle without stopping.
- Tell what your folk song is about.
- Observation: does the group keep together?

Teacher notes (demo)
Folk songs from children's families make music lessons meaningful.
