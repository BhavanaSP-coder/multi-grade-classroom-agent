Subject: Kannada
Grade: 2
Unit: ವ್ಯಂಜನಗಳು ಮತ್ತು ಕಾಗುಣಿತ (Consonants and Vowel Signs)

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Recognises and writes the Kannada consonants (ವ್ಯಂಜನಗಳು).
- Forms ಕಾಗುಣಿತ: adds vowel signs to consonants (ಕ ಕಾ ಕಿ ಕೀ ...).
- Reads two- and three-letter words such as ನೀರು, ಹಸು, ನಾಯಿ, ಬೆಕ್ಕು.
- Copies short words neatly.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Kagunita chart: read ಕ ಕಾ ಕಿ ಕೀ aloud row by row.
2. Letter cards: make words by joining cards (ಹ + ಸು = ಹಸು).
3. Word ladder: change one letter to make a new word.
4. Animal words: draw an animal and write its name.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Write the ಕಾಗುಣಿತ of ಮ.
- Read and draw: ನಾಯಿ, ಬೆಕ್ಕು.
- Fill in the missing letter: ನೀ__.

Teacher notes (demo)
Practise a few consonants each day; revise earlier letters often.
