Subject: Physical Education
Grade: 5
Unit: Fitness Components and Kabaddi

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Names fitness components: strength, flexibility, speed and endurance.
- Measures their pulse before and after exercise.
- Plays kabaddi with basic rules and safe tackling.
- Sets a simple personal fitness goal.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Fitness stations: skipping, push-ups against a wall, sit-and-reach, shuttle run.
2. Pulse check: count heartbeats for 15 seconds before and after running.
3. Kabaddi basics: raiding, chanting and safe holding.
4. Goal card: each child writes one fitness goal for the month.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Record your pulse before and after running.
- Name two fitness components.
- Explain one rule of kabaddi.

Teacher notes (demo)
Teach safe tackling before any kabaddi match; supervise closely.
