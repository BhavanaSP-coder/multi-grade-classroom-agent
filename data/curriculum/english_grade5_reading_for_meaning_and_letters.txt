Subject: English
Grade: 5
Unit: Reading for Meaning and Letter Writing

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Reads longer texts and identifies the main idea and key details.
- Uses adjectives and adverbs to make sentences more interesting.
- Writes an informal letter with date, greeting, body and closing.
- Speaks for one minute on a familiar topic.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Main idea hunt: groups read a passage and agree on one sentence that sums it up.
2. Sentence makeover: add an adjective and an adverb to a plain sentence.
3. Letter writing: write a letter to a friend about the school annual day.
4. One-minute talk: each child speaks about 'My favourite place'.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Write the main idea of the passage in one sentence.
- Add an adverb: The boy ran ____.
- Write the format of an informal letter.

Teacher notes (demo)
Give a model letter first and discuss its parts before students write.
