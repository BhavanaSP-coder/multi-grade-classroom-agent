Subject: Music
Grade: 5
Unit: Patriotic Songs and Making Simple Tunes

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Sings the national anthem correctly and stands in attention.
- Sings patriotic songs in a group with clear words.
- Creates a short tune using three or four swaras.
- Groups instruments into string, wind and percussion.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Anthem practice: correct posture and timing (about 52 seconds).
2. Patriotic song practice for Independence Day.
3. Tune making: groups make a four-note tune and sing it to the class.
4. Instrument families sort with picture cards.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Sing the national anthem with correct posture.
- Sing your group's tune.
- Sort: flute, tabla, veena, drum into instrument families.

Teacher notes (demo)
Teach the meaning of the anthem's words along with the singing.
