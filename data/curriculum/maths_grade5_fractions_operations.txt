Subject: Mathematics
Grade: 5
Unit: Fractions - Addition, Subtraction and Applications

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Adds and subtracts fractions with the same denominator (2/7 + 3/7 = 5/7).
- Adds and subtracts simple fractions with different denominators by using equivalent fractions (1/2 + 1/4 = 3/4).
- Converts between improper fractions and mixed numbers (7/4 = 1 3/4).
- Relates fractions to decimals for tenths and halves (1/2 = 0.5, 3/10 = 0.3).
- Solves multi-step word problems on fractions in contexts such as farming, cooking, money and distance, and explains the steps in writing.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Farm plot problem: draw a field on the blackboard divided into 8 equal plots. 3/8 has ragi, 2/8 has groundnut. What fraction is planted? What fraction is empty?
2. Recipe fractions: a recipe needs 1/2 cup of dal and 1/4 cup of rice. How much in total? Use paper strips to add.
3. Mixed number line: draw a number line from 0 to 3 marked in quarters. Children locate 5/4, 1 1/2 and 9/4.
4. Peer teaching: Grade 5 students explain one fraction problem to a younger student using a drawing (useful in multi-grade rooms).

Assessment ideas (demo)
- 3/10 + 4/10 = ?
- 1/2 + 1/4 = ? Show with a drawing.
- Write 11/4 as a mixed number.
- A farmer used 2/5 of his land for paddy and 1/5 for vegetables. What fraction of the land is left?

Teacher notes (demo)
Before adding fractions with different denominators, revise equivalent fractions from Grade 4. Ask students to write one sentence explaining each step. Grade 5 students can act as peer helpers for younger grades during independent work time.
