Subject: Social Studies
Grade: 4
Unit: Maps, Directions and Our State

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Uses the four main directions (North, South, East, West) to locate places.
- Reads a simple map using a key and symbols.
- Locates their district and state on a map of India.
- Names the capital and some districts of their state.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Direction game: stand in the ground facing the sunrise and name the directions.
2. Map key: make symbols for river, road, railway, school.
3. State puzzle: cut an outline map into districts and reassemble it.
4. Find on map: locate the state capital and the nearest river.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- In which direction does the sun rise?
- Use the map key to find the railway line.
- Name the capital of your state.

Teacher notes (demo)
Use the state map available in school. Teachers should add local districts and rivers.
