Subject: Physical Education
Grade: 3
Unit: Traditional Games and Fitness

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Plays traditional games such as lagori (seven stones) and kho-kho following rules.
- Runs short distances with speed.
- Does simple warm-up and cool-down exercises.
- Works as a team.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Lagori: knock down the stone pile and rebuild it while the other team tries to tag you.
2. Kho-kho introduction: practise chasing and giving 'kho'.
3. Warm-up routine: jogging, arm circles, toe touches.
4. Short sprints of 20-30 metres.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Explain one rule of lagori.
- Observation: does the child warm up properly?
- Run 30 metres; record the time if a watch is available.

Teacher notes (demo)
Traditional games need no equipment and build teamwork.
