Subject: Social Studies
Grade: 2
Unit: My Neighbourhood and Community Helpers

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Names important places in the neighbourhood: market, hospital, post office, temple, bus stand.
- Names community helpers and the work they do (farmer, doctor, postman, shopkeeper).
- Explains how people in a community depend on each other.
- Shows respect for all kinds of work.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Helper riddles: describe a helper's work and children guess who it is.
2. Neighbourhood walk: note three places near the school.
3. Role play: a market with shopkeepers and buyers.
4. Thank-you card for a community helper.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Match the helper to the tool: farmer - plough, doctor - stethoscope.
- Draw one place in your neighbourhood and write its name.
- Oral: How does a farmer help us?

Teacher notes (demo)
Value every kind of work equally; children's parents may do any of these jobs.
