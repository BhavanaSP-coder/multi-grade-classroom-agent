Subject: Kannada
Grade: 5
Unit: ಪತ್ರ ಲೇಖನ ಮತ್ತು ಗಾದೆಗಳು (Letter Writing and Proverbs)

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Writes a short letter (ಪತ್ರ) to a family member or friend in the correct format.
- Explains the meaning of common ಗಾದೆಗಳು (proverbs) with examples.
- Reads a lesson and identifies its main idea.
- Writes a short paragraph (ಪ್ರಬಂಧ) on a familiar topic.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Letter model: read a sample letter and label date, greeting, body and ending.
2. Proverb stories: groups act out a situation that explains a ಗಾದೆ.
3. Main idea: groups write one sentence that sums up the lesson.
4. Paragraph on 'ನಮ್ಮ ಊರು' (our village).

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Write a letter to your grandmother about your school.
- Explain one ಗಾದೆ in your own words.
- Write five sentences about ನಮ್ಮ ಊರು.

Teacher notes (demo)
Invite elders to share proverbs; it connects school language with home and culture.
