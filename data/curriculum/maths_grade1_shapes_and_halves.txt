Subject: Mathematics
Grade: 1
Unit: Shapes, Sharing and the Idea of "Half"

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Recognises and names basic shapes (circle, square, triangle, rectangle) in objects around the classroom and home.
- Sorts objects by shape, size and colour.
- Shares a small group of objects (up to 10) equally between two children and says whether the sharing is "fair" or "not fair".
- Understands "whole" and "half" using real objects: half a roti, half a banana, half a sheet of paper.
- Uses words such as whole, half, equal, more, less, same.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Fair sharing with stones: give pairs of children 6, 8 or 10 pebbles or seeds. Ask them to share so each child has the same number. Children count aloud to check.
2. Paper folding: fold a square piece of old newspaper into two equal parts. Colour or mark one part. Say "This is one half."
3. Roti story: the teacher draws a circle (roti) on the blackboard, draws a line through the middle and asks, "If two friends share this roti fairly, how much does each get?"
4. Shape walk: children find circles, squares and triangles in the classroom (clock, window, roof shape) and draw them on their slates.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Oral: "Show me half of this paper." "Is this fair sharing?"
- Picture task: circle the pictures that show two equal parts.
- Children at this level answer orally or by drawing; avoid long written questions.

Teacher notes (demo)
Grade 1 children learn best through touching and moving objects. Keep instructions to one step at a time. Fraction symbols such as 1/2 are not introduced formally; use the word "half" and pictures.
