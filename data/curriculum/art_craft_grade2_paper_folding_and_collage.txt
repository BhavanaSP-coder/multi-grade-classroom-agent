Subject: Art and Craft
Grade: 2
Unit: Paper Folding and Collage

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Folds paper along lines to make simple shapes (fan, boat, cap).
- Makes a collage using seeds, leaves and paper scraps.
- Follows step-by-step craft instructions.
- Keeps materials tidy and shares them.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Paper boat: fold old newspaper into a boat and float it.
2. Seed collage: stick seeds on cardboard to make an animal outline.
3. Fan folding: make a hand fan for summer.
4. Gallery walk: display work and children say what they like about a friend's work.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Make a paper cap following the steps.
- Name the materials in your collage.
- Oral: What did you like about your friend's work?

Teacher notes (demo)
Demonstrate every fold slowly and let children copy step by step.
