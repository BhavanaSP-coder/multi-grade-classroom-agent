Subject: Environmental Studies (EVS)
Grade: 5
Unit: Our Natural Resources: Water, Soil and Forests

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Explains why forests are important for people, animals and rain.
- Describes how seeds grow into plants and what they need to germinate.
- Explains soil erosion in simple words and ways farmers protect soil.
- Suggests practical ways the school and village can conserve water and trees.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Germination experiment: grow green gram seeds on wet cloth and record growth daily for a week.
2. Erosion model: pour water on a slope of bare soil and on a slope covered with grass; compare the result.
3. Forest products list: groups list things that come from forests (wood, honey, medicines, fruits).
4. Plan a school water-saving campaign with posters for the village.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Draw the stages of a germinating seed and label them.
- Why does soil wash away faster where there are no plants?
- Write two ways to save water in school.

Teacher notes (demo)
Grade 5 students can record observations in a table over several days. Link with Science and Social Studies lessons.
