Subject: Hindi
Grade: 4
Unit: सर्वनाम और क्रिया (Pronouns and Verbs)

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Identifies संज्ञा, सर्वनाम (pronouns) and क्रिया (verbs) in sentences.
- Changes words between एकवचन and बहुवचन (singular and plural).
- Reads a lesson and answers questions in full sentences.
- Uses new words in their own sentences.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Replace the noun: change 'सीता खेलती है' to 'वह खेलती है'.
2. Verb mime: a child acts खाना or दौड़ना and others write the verb.
3. Plural game: teacher says लड़का, children say लड़के.
4. Word wall of new words from each lesson.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Underline the सर्वनाम: वह स्कूल जाता है।
- Write the plural of किताब and लड़की.
- Answer two questions about the lesson.

Teacher notes (demo)
Link grammar to sentences children already use; avoid long rule lists.
