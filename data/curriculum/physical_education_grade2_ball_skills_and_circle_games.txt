Subject: Physical Education
Grade: 2
Unit: Ball Skills and Circle Games

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Throws and catches a soft ball with two hands.
- Kicks a ball towards a target.
- Plays circle games following rules and taking turns.
- Shows good sportsmanship.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Pair catching with a soft or paper ball.
2. Target kick: kick a ball between two stones.
3. Circle games such as passing the ball around a circle.
4. Cool down: slow breathing and stretching.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Observation: catches the ball 3 times out of 5.
- Kick the ball between the stones.
- Oral: Why do we take turns in games?

Teacher notes (demo)
A ball made of rolled paper and string works well when equipment is limited.
