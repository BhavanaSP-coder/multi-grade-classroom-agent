Subject: Science
Grade: 4
Unit: Food and Nutrition

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Groups foods into energy-giving, body-building and protective foods.
- Explains what a balanced meal is using local foods.
- Describes how to keep teeth healthy and why we chew food well.
- Explains simple ways to keep food safe from flies and spoiling.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Plate planner: groups use drawings of local foods to make a balanced plate (ragi, rice, dal, vegetables, curd).
2. Midday meal check: list the food groups in today's school meal.
3. Teeth count: children count their teeth with a small mirror and learn their kinds.
4. Spoiling test: keep a slice of fruit covered and one uncovered for a day and compare.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Write two foods each for energy, body-building and protection.
- Is rice with dal and vegetables a balanced meal? Explain.
- Why should food be covered?

Teacher notes (demo)
Be sensitive: families eat different foods. Use only locally available foods as examples.
