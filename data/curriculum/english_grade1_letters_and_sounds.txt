Subject: English
Grade: 1
Unit: Letters, Sounds and Greetings

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Recognises capital and small letters a-z.
- Says the sound of each letter (phonics) and the first sound of simple words.
- Uses greetings: Good morning, Thank you, Please, Sorry.
- Listens to a short rhyme and says it with actions.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Letter hunt: find letters on charts, packets and signboards around school.
2. Sound sorting: children sort picture cards by first sound (b - bat, ball, bus).
3. Sand tray writing: trace letters in sand or on the floor with a finger.
4. Greeting role play: children greet each other at the start and end of the day.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Point to the letter the teacher says.
- Oral: What is the first sound in 'cat'?
- Match small letters to capital letters.

Teacher notes (demo)
Use the children's home language to explain, then repeat in English. Keep sessions short and lively.
