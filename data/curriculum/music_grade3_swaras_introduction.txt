Subject: Music
Grade: 3
Unit: Introduction to Swaras

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Sings the seven swaras Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Da Ni in ascending and descending order.
- Sings simple group songs in tune.
- Recognises some Indian musical instruments by sight and sound.
- Follows a leader while singing.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Swara ladder: sing up and down the swaras while stepping up and down.
2. Echo singing: teacher sings a short phrase, children repeat it.
3. Instrument pictures: match instruments with their sounds (veena, flute, tabla, mridangam).
4. Group song practice for the school assembly.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Sing Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Da Ni.
- Name two Indian instruments.
- Observation: does the child sing in tune with the group?

Teacher notes (demo)
Keep the pitch comfortable for children's voices.
