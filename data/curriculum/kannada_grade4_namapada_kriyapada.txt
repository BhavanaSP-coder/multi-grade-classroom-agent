Subject: Kannada
Grade: 4
Unit: ನಾಮಪದ ಮತ್ತು ಕ್ರಿಯಾಪದ (Nouns and Verbs)

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Identifies ನಾಮಪದ (nouns) and ಕ್ರಿಯಾಪದ (verbs) in sentences.
- Changes words between ಏಕವಚನ and ಬಹುವಚನ (singular and plural).
- Reads a lesson and answers questions in full sentences.
- Uses new words from the lesson in their own sentences.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Word sort: sort word cards into ನಾಮಪದ and ಕ್ರಿಯಾಪದ.
2. Singular-plural game: teacher says ಮರ, children say ಮರಗಳು.
3. Act the verb: a child acts ಓಡು or ತಿನ್ನು and others write the word.
4. New word wall: add five new words from each lesson.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Underline the ನಾಮಪದ in two sentences.
- Write the plural of ಹಸು and ಮನೆ.
- Answer two questions about the lesson in full sentences.

Teacher notes (demo)
Use examples from children's own speech to explain grammar terms.
