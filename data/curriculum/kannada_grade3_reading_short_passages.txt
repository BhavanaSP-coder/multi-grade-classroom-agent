Subject: Kannada
Grade: 3
Unit: ಸಣ್ಣ ಪಾಠಗಳ ಓದು (Reading Short Passages)

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Reads short passages and poems aloud with correct pronunciation.
- Recognises simple ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರ (conjunct letters) in common words such as ಅಮ್ಮ, ಹಣ್ಣು.
- Answers simple questions about a passage.
- Writes three or four simple sentences.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Echo reading: teacher reads a line and children repeat it.
2. Ottakshara hunt: underline words with ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರ in a passage.
3. Question chain: each child asks the next child one question about the story.
4. Picture sentences: write a sentence for each of three pictures.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Read the passage aloud to the teacher.
- Write two words that have ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರ.
- Answer: ಕಥೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಯಾರು ಇದ್ದರು?

Teacher notes (demo)
Pair stronger readers with children who need support during reading time.
