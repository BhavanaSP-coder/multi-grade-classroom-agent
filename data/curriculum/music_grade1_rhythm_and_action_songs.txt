Subject: Music
Grade: 1
Unit: Rhythm and Action Songs

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Claps and stamps to a steady beat.
- Sings simple action songs and rhymes in a group.
- Tells loud sounds from soft sounds.
- Listens quietly to music for a short time.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Clap the beat: clap along with a song while the teacher keeps time on a drum or table.
2. Action songs with movements.
3. Loud and soft game: sing loudly when the teacher raises hands, softly when hands go down.
4. Listening corner: identify sounds of the village (bird, bell, cow).

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Observation: can the child clap a steady beat?
- Oral: Was that sound loud or soft?
- Sing one rhyme with actions.

Teacher notes (demo)
Use local songs and rhymes children already know.
