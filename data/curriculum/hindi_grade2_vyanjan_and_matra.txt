Subject: Hindi
Grade: 2
Unit: व्यंजन और मात्राएँ (Consonants and Vowel Signs)

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Recognises and writes common Hindi consonants (व्यंजन).
- Reads words without vowel signs such as कमल, नल, जल, घर.
- Adds मात्राएँ (vowel signs) to consonants: क का कि की.
- Reads simple words with मात्राएँ such as पानी, माला.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Word building: join letter cards to make कमल and नल.
2. Matra wheel: turn a paper wheel to change the मात्रा on a letter.
3. Read and act: read a word and show its meaning.
4. Copy writing: copy five words neatly.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Read the words: जल, नल, घर.
- Add the आ मात्रा to क, ग, म.
- Draw पानी and write the word.

Teacher notes (demo)
Start with words without मात्राएँ, then add one मात्रा at a time.
