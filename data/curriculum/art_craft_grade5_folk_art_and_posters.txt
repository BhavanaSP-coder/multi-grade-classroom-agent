Subject: Art and Craft
Grade: 5
Unit: Folk Art Patterns and Poster Making

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Recognises features of Indian folk art styles such as Warli patterns.
- Creates artwork inspired by a folk style using simple shapes.
- Designs a poster with a clear message, picture and few words.
- Gives and receives feedback on artwork respectfully.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Warli-style figures: draw people and animals with triangles, circles and lines on brown paper.
2. Local art talk: invite a local artisan or look at local wall paintings.
3. Poster: design a poster on 'Save Water' for the village.
4. Peer feedback: two stars and one wish for a friend's poster.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Draw a Warli-style scene of a village festival.
- Make a poster with a message of no more than six words.
- Write one good thing about a friend's poster.

Teacher notes (demo)
Respect the cultural origins of folk art; explain where the style comes from.
