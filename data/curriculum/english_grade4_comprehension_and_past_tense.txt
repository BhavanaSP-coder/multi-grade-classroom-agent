Subject: English
Grade: 4
Unit: Reading Comprehension and the Past Tense

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Reads a passage and answers factual and simple inference questions.
- Uses the simple past tense of regular and common irregular verbs (walked, went, ate).
- Uses capital letters, full stops and question marks correctly.
- Writes a short paragraph about a family event.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Yesterday talk: children say what they did yesterday using past tense verbs.
2. Verb pairs: match present and past forms (go - went, play - played).
3. Punctuation fix: correct a paragraph written without capital letters and full stops.
4. Paragraph frame: write about a festival using a frame (First... Then... Finally...).

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Change to past tense: I play in the field.
- Put the punctuation: where is my book
- Read the passage and answer two questions.

Teacher notes (demo)
Read passages aloud first for weaker readers, then let them read in pairs.
