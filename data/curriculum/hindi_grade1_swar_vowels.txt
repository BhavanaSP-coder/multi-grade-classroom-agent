Subject: Hindi
Grade: 1
Unit: स्वर (Hindi Vowels)

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Recognises and says the Hindi vowels अ आ इ ई उ ऊ ऋ ए ऐ ओ औ.
- Writes the vowels with correct shape.
- Recognises simple words such as कमल, जल, घर.
- Recites short Hindi rhymes with actions.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Vowel chart song: sing and point to each vowel.
2. Air writing: children write the vowel in the air and then on the slate.
3. Picture match: match pictures to words like कमल and घर.
4. Rhyme with actions.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Oral: say the vowel the teacher points to.
- Write अ आ इ ई on the slate.
- Circle the picture whose name begins with क.

Teacher notes (demo)
Hindi may be a new language for many children here; use gestures and pictures and do not expect fast progress.
