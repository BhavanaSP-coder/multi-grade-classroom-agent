Subject: Environmental Studies (EVS)
Grade: 1
Unit: My Body, My Family and Keeping Clean

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Names the main parts of the body and says what each part helps us do.
- Names family members and describes how they help one another at home.
- Follows simple cleanliness habits: washing hands, brushing teeth, bathing.
- Tells the difference between clean and dirty places and why cleanliness matters.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Touch and tell: the teacher names a body part and children touch it and say what it does (eyes - see, ears - hear).
2. Family circle: children draw their family on a slate and tell who does which work at home.
3. Handwashing song: practise the six steps of washing hands with soap or ash at the school tap.
4. Clean or dirty: show pictures or real places in school and children sort them into clean and dirty.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Oral: Which body part do we use to smell? To walk?
- Picture task: circle the good habits (brushing teeth, throwing waste in a bin).
- Observation: does the child wash hands before the midday meal?

Teacher notes (demo)
Grade 1 children learn through doing and talking. Avoid long written work; use pictures, songs and real practice.
