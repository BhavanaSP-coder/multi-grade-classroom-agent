Subject: Physical Education
Grade: 1
Unit: Basic Movements and Playground Safety

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Walks, runs, hops, jumps and balances with control.
- Follows simple instructions during play.
- Plays safely without hurting others.
- Enjoys moving with classmates.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Animal walks: move like a frog, an elephant and a rabbit.
2. Follow the leader along a line.
3. Balance game: stand on one foot while counting to five.
4. Freeze game: move to a clap and freeze when it stops.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Observation: can the child hop on one foot five times?
- Oral: What should we do before running in the ground?
- Observation: does the child follow the rules of the game?

Teacher notes (demo)
Check the ground for stones and thorns before every session.
