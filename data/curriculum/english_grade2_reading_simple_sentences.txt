Subject: English
Grade: 2
Unit: Reading Simple Sentences

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Reads two- and three-letter words and common sight words (the, is, a, my).
- Reads simple sentences such as 'The cat is on the mat.'
- Identifies naming words (nouns) for people, animals, places and things.
- Writes simple words and short sentences with support.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Word families: build words with -at (cat, hat, mat, bat) using letter cards.
2. Sentence strips: children arrange word cards to make sentences.
3. Naming word walk: list names of things seen in the classroom.
4. Rhyme time: read a short rhyme together and underline rhyming words.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Read the sentence and draw the picture.
- Circle the naming words: The boy has a red ball.
- Write two words that rhyme with 'pin'.

Teacher notes (demo)
Model reading aloud slowly with a finger under each word.
