Subject: Science
Grade: 2
Unit: What Plants Need to Grow

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Names what plants need to grow: water, sunlight, air and soil.
- Describes how a plant changes as it grows.
- Compares a plant kept in sunlight with one kept in a dark place.
- Records simple observations with drawings.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Two-pot test: keep one plant in sunlight and one under a box for a week; compare their colour and height.
2. Water or no water: grow seeds in two cups, water only one, and observe.
3. Plant diary: draw the plant every two days.
4. Shadow and sun walk: notice where plants grow well in the school ground.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Draw the plant kept in the dark and the plant kept in the sun.
- Oral: What will happen if a plant gets no water?
- Tick the things a plant needs: water, sunlight, toys, air.

Teacher notes (demo)
Let children predict before they observe. Use the word 'predict' and praise careful observation.
