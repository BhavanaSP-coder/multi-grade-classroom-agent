Subject: Social Studies
Grade: 1
Unit: Me and My School

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Tells their name, age, class and school name.
- Names people who work in the school and how they help.
- Follows simple school rules and explains why rules help everyone.
- Takes turns and shares in group activities.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. All about me: children draw themselves and say three things about themselves.
2. School helpers tour: meet the cook, cleaner and head teacher and ask one question.
3. Class rules chart: children suggest three rules and draw a picture for each.
4. Sharing game: pass a ball and share one thing you like.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Oral: Who cooks our midday meal?
- Circle the good behaviour in the pictures.
- Tell one school rule and why it is important.

Teacher notes (demo)
In many state syllabi Grades 1-2 cover this inside EVS. Keep it oral and activity-based.
