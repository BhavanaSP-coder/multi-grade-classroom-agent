Subject: Art and Craft
Grade: 4
Unit: Drawing from Observation and Best Out of Waste

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Draws objects by observing their shape, size and shading.
- Makes useful items from waste materials.
- Plans a craft by listing materials and steps.
- Explains why reusing waste helps the environment.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Still life: draw a pot and a fruit placed on a table.
2. Bottle craft: make a pen stand or planter from a plastic bottle.
3. Plan sheet: write materials and steps before making a craft.
4. Light and shadow: shade one side of a drawn ball.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Draw a leaf you observed with its veins.
- List the materials and steps for your waste craft.
- Oral: How does reusing waste help our village?

Teacher notes (demo)
Collect clean waste materials a week before the activity.
