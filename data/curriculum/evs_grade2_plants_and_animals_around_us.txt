Subject: Environmental Studies (EVS)
Grade: 2
Unit: Plants and Animals Around Us

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Identifies common plants and trees in the school and village by name.
- Names the parts of a plant: root, stem, leaf, flower, fruit.
- Groups animals as pet, farm and wild animals and says where they live.
- Shows care for plants and animals: watering plants, not hurting animals.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Plant walk: walk around the school, collect fallen leaves and compare their shapes and sizes.
2. Uproot a small weed carefully and show the root, stem and leaves; children draw and label it.
3. Animal sounds game: one child makes an animal sound and others guess and say where it lives.
4. Adopt a plant: each group waters one plant daily and reports how it changes.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Label the parts of a plant in a drawing.
- Sort picture cards: cow, dog, tiger, hen, elephant, cat into pet, farm and wild.
- Oral: What will happen to a plant if we do not water it?

Teacher notes (demo)
Use real plants and animals from the village. Children may know local names; accept them and add the common name.
