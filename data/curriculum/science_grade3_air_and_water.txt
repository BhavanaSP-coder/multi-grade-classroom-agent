Subject: Science
Grade: 3
Unit: Air and Water Around Us

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Shows that air is everywhere and takes up space.
- Describes three forms of water: ice, water and steam.
- Explains that wet clothes dry because water evaporates.
- Identifies things that float and sink in water.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Air takes space: push an upturned glass into a bucket of water and see that water does not fill it.
2. Ice to water to steam: watch ice melt in a plate and a covered pot of water steaming during cooking (teacher only).
3. Drying race: hang two wet cloths, one in sun and one in shade, and compare drying time.
4. Float or sink: test a stone, leaf, cork, coin and wooden stick in a bucket.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Name the three forms of water with one example of each.
- Why does a wet cloth dry faster in the sun?
- Sort: stone, leaf, coin, wooden stick into float or sink.

Teacher notes (demo)
Do all heating demonstrations yourself and keep children at a safe distance.
