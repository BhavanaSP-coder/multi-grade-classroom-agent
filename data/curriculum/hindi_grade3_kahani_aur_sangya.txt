Subject: Hindi
Grade: 3
Unit: कहानी और संज्ञा (Stories and Nouns)

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Reads short stories and poems in Hindi with understanding.
- Identifies संज्ञा (nouns) for people, places and things.
- Answers simple questions about a story.
- Writes three simple sentences in Hindi.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Story reading: teacher reads, children repeat each sentence.
2. Noun hunt: underline संज्ञा words in the story.
3. Who, where, what: children answer questions in pairs.
4. Picture sentences: write one sentence for each picture.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Underline the संज्ञा: राम बाज़ार गया।
- Answer: कहानी में कौन था?
- Write a sentence about your school.

Teacher notes (demo)
Read slowly and check understanding often; explain new words with pictures.
