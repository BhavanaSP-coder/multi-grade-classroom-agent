Subject: Science
Grade: 1
Unit: Living and Non-living Things

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Sorts things around us into living and non-living.
- Says that living things eat, grow, breathe and move.
- Names a few things that were living once (dry leaf, wood).
- Observes and describes things using the senses.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Sorting game: children collect objects from the school ground (stone, leaf, ant, chalk) and sort them.
2. Does it grow? Compare a growing plant with a stone over two weeks.
3. Feel and tell: a bag with objects; children touch one and describe it before seeing it.
4. Picture talk: show a village scene and children point to living things.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Circle the living things in the picture.
- Oral: Is a stone living? How do you know?
- Name two things that need food.

Teacher notes (demo)
In many state syllabi Grades 1-2 cover this inside EVS. Keep it practical and oral.
