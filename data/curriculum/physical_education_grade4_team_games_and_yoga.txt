Subject: Physical Education
Grade: 4
Unit: Team Games, Relays and Yoga

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Plays team games and relays with fair play.
- Performs simple yoga asanas such as Tadasana and Vrikshasana.
- Understands the importance of warming up and drinking water.
- Encourages team members.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Relay race with a baton (a stick) in teams.
2. Yoga session: Tadasana and Vrikshasana with slow breathing.
3. Team game with simple rules decided by the class.
4. Discuss: how do we feel after exercise?

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Hold Vrikshasana for 10 counts.
- Oral: Why should we drink water after playing?
- Observation: does the child encourage teammates?

Teacher notes (demo)
Never force a yoga pose; children should stop if they feel pain.
