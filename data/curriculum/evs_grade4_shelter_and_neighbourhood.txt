Subject: Environmental Studies (EVS)
Grade: 4
Unit: Shelter, Travel and Our Neighbourhood

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Describes different kinds of houses and the materials used to build them.
- Explains how animals and birds make their homes (nests, burrows, hives).
- Names ways people travel and carry goods, and compares them by speed and cost.
- Reads a simple picture map of the neighbourhood and finds places on it.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. House survey: children note what their house roof and walls are made of; make a class tally chart.
2. Nest watch: observe a bird's nest from a distance and draw the materials used.
3. Travel line: arrange cards of bullock cart, bicycle, bus, train and aeroplane from slowest to fastest.
4. Draw the route from home to school and mark three landmarks.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Match the animal to its home: bird - nest, bee - hive, rabbit - burrow.
- Which house material is best for a rainy place? Give one reason.
- Find the post office on the class map and say what is next to it.

Teacher notes (demo)
Respect all kinds of homes. Never compare children's houses in a way that embarrasses anyone.
