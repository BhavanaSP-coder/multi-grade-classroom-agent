Subject: Art and Craft
Grade: 1
Unit: Colours, Shapes and Printing

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Names primary colours and finds them in nature.
- Draws basic shapes and uses them to make simple pictures.
- Makes leaf and finger prints using natural or simple colours.
- Talks about their own artwork.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Colour hunt: find red, yellow and blue things in the school ground.
2. Shape pictures: make a house, tree and sun using circles, squares and triangles.
3. Leaf printing with colour or turmeric and water.
4. Show and tell: each child explains their picture.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Name three colours you used.
- Draw a picture using only shapes.
- Observation: does the child handle materials safely?

Teacher notes (demo)
Value effort and imagination, not neatness. Use free local materials.
