Subject: Hindi
Grade: 5
Unit: पत्र लेखन और मुहावरे (Letter Writing and Idioms)

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Writes a short informal letter (पत्र) in the correct format.
- Uses common मुहावरे (idioms) in sentences with their meaning.
- Writes a short paragraph (अनुच्छेद) on a familiar topic.
- Uses विशेषण (adjectives) to describe people and things.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Letter model: read a sample letter and label its parts.
2. Idiom act: groups act out a मुहावरा for others to guess.
3. Describe a friend using three विशेषण.
4. Paragraph on 'मेरा गाँव' (my village).

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Write a letter to your friend about a festival.
- Use one मुहावरा in a sentence.
- Write five sentences on मेरा गाँव.

Teacher notes (demo)
Give a model paragraph first and discuss how it begins and ends.
