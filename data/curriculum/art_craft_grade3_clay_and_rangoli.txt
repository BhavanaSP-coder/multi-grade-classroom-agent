Subject: Art and Craft
Grade: 3
Unit: Clay Modelling and Rangoli Patterns

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Shapes clay into simple objects (pot, fruit, animal).
- Creates symmetrical rangoli patterns using dots and lines.
- Identifies patterns in local crafts and nature.
- Uses tools and materials safely.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Clay fruits: model three fruits using local clay or dough.
2. Dot rangoli: draw a dot grid on the ground and join dots to make a symmetrical pattern.
3. Pattern walk: find patterns on leaves, cloth and walls.
4. Small pot making: pinch-pot method.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Make a symmetrical pattern on a 5 by 5 dot grid.
- Show and name your clay model.
- Draw one pattern you saw in nature.

Teacher notes (demo)
Rangoli links with Maths symmetry; coordinate with the Maths teacher.
