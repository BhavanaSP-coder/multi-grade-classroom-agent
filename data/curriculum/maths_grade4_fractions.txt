Subject: Mathematics
Grade: 4
Unit: Fractions - Equivalence, Comparison and Number Line

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Identifies equivalent fractions using paper folding and drawings (1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8; 1/3 = 2/6).
- Compares and orders fractions with the same denominator (3/8 < 5/8) and unit fractions (1/5 < 1/3).
- Places simple fractions (halves, quarters, eighths) on a number line between 0 and 1.
- Finds a fraction of a quantity: 3/4 of 20 rupees, 1/5 of 30 minutes.
- Solves simple word problems involving fractions of money, time and length from daily life.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Fraction wall on the blackboard: draw strips for 1 whole, halves, quarters and eighths, one under another. Children find fractions that are the same length (equivalent).
2. Rope number line: tie a rope or draw a long chalk line from 0 to 1. Children stand at 1/2, 1/4, 3/4 holding cards.
3. Market problems: "Amma bought 2 kg of rice and used 1/4 of it. How much rice is used?" Children solve with drawings.
4. Equivalence by folding: fold paper into halves, then fold again. Show that 1 half covers the same area as 2 quarters.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Fill in: 1/2 = ?/4 = ?/8.
- Put these in order from smallest to biggest: 5/8, 1/8, 3/8.
- Mark 3/4 on a number line.
- Ravi walked 3/4 of a 4 km road. How far did he walk?

Teacher notes (demo)
Children often think 1/8 is bigger than 1/4 because 8 is bigger than 4. Use the fraction wall to correct this. Link fractions to money and time so the ideas feel useful.
