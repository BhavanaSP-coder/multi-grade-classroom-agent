Subject: Computer Science
Grade: 1
Unit: What Is a Computer?

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Identifies a computer and its main parts: monitor, keyboard, mouse, CPU.
- Names places where computers are used: bank, shop, hospital, school.
- Tells the difference between a computer and other machines.
- Handles devices gently when available.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Picture cards: match each computer part to its name.
2. Paper keyboard: children press letters on a drawn keyboard.
3. Where are computers? Children share where they have seen computers or mobile phones.
4. Machine sorting: fan, computer, bicycle, mobile phone.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Point to the mouse in the picture.
- Oral: Where have you seen a computer?
- Name two parts of a computer.

Teacher notes (demo)
These lessons work without a computer; use pictures and paper models if no device is available.
