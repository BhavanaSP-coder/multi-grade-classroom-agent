Subject: Science
Grade: 5
Unit: The Water Cycle and Weather

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Explains evaporation and condensation using daily-life examples.
- Draws and explains the water cycle: sea, clouds, rain, rivers.
- Measures rainfall with a simple rain gauge.
- Relates weather changes to farming activities in the village.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Condensation demo: hold a cold steel plate over a vessel of hot water and observe droplets.
2. Water cycle in a bag: water in a sealed plastic bag taped to a sunny window; observe drops forming.
3. Rain gauge: make one from a bottle and a scale and record rainfall for a week.
4. Farmer interview: ask how rain decides the sowing season.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Label the water cycle: evaporation, condensation, rain.
- Why do drops form on a cold glass of water?
- Read the class rainfall table and say which day had most rain.

Teacher notes (demo)
Let students explain the water cycle to younger grades as peer teachers.
