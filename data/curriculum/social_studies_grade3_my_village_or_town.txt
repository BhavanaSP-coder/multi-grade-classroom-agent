Subject: Social Studies
Grade: 3
Unit: My Village or Town: Transport, Communication and Festivals

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Describes their village or town: important places, work people do.
- Names means of transport and communication and how they have changed.
- Describes festivals celebrated in the village and what they share.
- Draws a simple map of the village with a key.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Then and now: compare a letter, phone call and mobile message.
2. Festival calendar: children list festivals by month with drawings.
3. Village map: groups draw a map and add symbols for school, temple, tank, road.
4. Interview an elder about how people travelled earlier.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Name two means of transport used in your village.
- Draw a symbol for a school and a hospital.
- Write two sentences about a festival you like.

Teacher notes (demo)
Celebrate all festivals children mention; avoid ranking one above another.
