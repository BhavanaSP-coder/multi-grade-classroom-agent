Subject: Kannada
Grade: 1
Unit: ಸ್ವರಗಳು (Kannada Vowels)

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Recognises and says the Kannada vowels ಅ ಆ ಇ ಈ ಉ ಊ ಋ ಎ ಏ ಐ ಒ ಓ ಔ.
- Writes the vowels with correct shape and direction.
- Recognises simple words such as ಅಮ್ಮ, ಅಪ್ಪ, ಮನೆ, ಮರ.
- Listens to and recites simple Kannada rhymes with actions.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Vowel song: sing the vowels together while pointing to a chart.
2. Sand tray: trace each vowel in sand or with a wet finger on the slate.
3. Picture match: match pictures of ಅಮ್ಮ and ಮನೆ to the first letter.
4. Rhyme with actions in a circle.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Oral: say the vowel the teacher points to.
- Write ಅ ಆ ಇ ಈ on the slate.
- Circle the word that starts with ಅ.

Teacher notes (demo)
Most children already speak Kannada at home; build on spoken words to teach letters.
