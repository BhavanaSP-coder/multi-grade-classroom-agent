Subject: English
Grade: 3
Unit: Short Stories, Naming and Action Words

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Reads a short story and answers simple questions about it.
- Identifies nouns and verbs (action words) in sentences.
- Uses describing words such as big, small, red, happy.
- Writes three or four sentences about a picture or about themselves.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Story circle: teacher reads a short story; children retell it in order using picture cards.
2. Action game: one child acts a verb (run, jump, eat) and others guess and write it.
3. Describe it: children describe an object from the bag using two describing words.
4. My day: children write four sentences about their day.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- Underline the action words: The girl runs and jumps.
- Answer: Who is the story about? What happened at the end?
- Write a sentence using the word 'happy'.

Teacher notes (demo)
Accept short answers in Grade 3 and gradually ask for full sentences.
