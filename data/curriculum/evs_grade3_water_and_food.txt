Subject: Environmental Studies (EVS)
Grade: 3
Unit: Water and Food in Our Lives

Learning outcomes (demo)
- Names sources of water in the village: well, tank, river, tap, rain.
- Explains why we must not waste water and suggests ways to save it.
- Groups foods that come from plants and foods that come from animals.
- Describes how some foods are cooked and why some foods are eaten raw.

Suggested low-resource activities (demo)
1. Water map: children draw where their family gets water and share it with the class.
2. Measure waste: leave a tap dripping into a mug for ten minutes and see how much water is wasted.
3. Food basket: children bring or name foods from home and sort them into plant food and animal food.
4. Interview: ask an elder how water was collected when they were young and compare with today.

Assessment ideas (demo)
- List three sources of water and one way to save water at home.
- Sort: rice, milk, egg, banana, curd, carrot into plant or animal food.
- Oral: Why should drinking water be covered?

Teacher notes (demo)
Connect lessons to the school midday meal and the village water source. Encourage children to share family practices.
